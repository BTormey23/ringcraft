Ringcraft — quick guide

Design stride-correct hunter, jumper and equitation courses; print show-ready plans; share live links and QR codes with riders.

Design a course in 30 seconds

Click + New course, pick the ring, discipline and level, and choose a course type — the designer lays out a stride-correct track. Don't like it? Regenerate deals a fresh layout, Mirror flips it. Then adjust by hand: drag any fence to move it, use the round handle to rotate (or press R for 15° steps), arrow keys nudge. Distances between related fences update live, in strides.

Edit fences & the track

Click a fence to edit everything in the right-hand panel: type, name, height, spread, notes, trot fence, combinations, and whether it's in the course. + Fence and − Fence add and remove. To bend a line by hand, hover a route segment and drag the waypoints that appear; Reset track returns to the automatic route. Measure lets you drag between any two points to read feet and strides.

One jump layout, many courses

Set your jumps once, then design courses for every level without moving a single standard: Save as layout stores the physical set. Starting a new course from that layout keeps every jump in place — use Route by clicking to pick which jumps are in this course and in what order, or Auto-route to let the designer choose. Unused jumps stay ghosted in the ring, exactly like a real show morning.

Print & PDF

Print / PDF makes 8.5×11 sheets: the rider plan (route, strides, table, time allowed), the jump crew sheet (every jump with heights, spreads and photos), and the judge / announcer sheet. Tick Print preview in the toolbar to see the sheet update live while you edit. When several courses share a layout, batch-print them all at once.

Share with riders

Mark a course Published to riders, then Share: the short link opens a live, phone-friendly view (edits appear when riders refresh); the QR code prints on rider plans and scans straight to it; the board link lists every published course — text it to the whole barn on show morning. A locked snapshot link never changes even if you edit later. Riders need no account and no app.

Yellow & red notices

Chips under the plan are advisory: distances that walk long or short, tight turns, inventory shortages, and Rule: notes based on typical USEF/FEI practice (combination distances, spreads vs height, effort counts). They never block you — schooling exercises break rules on purpose. Turn Rulebook checks off per course under Course details.

Your site, your data

On your own website, every Save publishes automatically — same courses on every device you've connected in Setup → Website. The site and its course data are public to anyone with the address; your GitHub token is not — it stays in your browser, so never paste it anywhere except Setup. Export backup in Setup makes a file copy any time.

Make it yours

In Setup → Stable & branding, set your stable's name, upload your logo and pick an accent color — they flow to the app, printed plans and rider pages. Running this for another barn? A fresh install gives them their own site; they brand it themselves in the same place.
