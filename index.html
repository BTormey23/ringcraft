Course design · Hunter / Jumper / Equitation

Courses that walk true.

RingCraft lays out stride-correct courses for your ring and your jumps, prints the rider, crew and judge sheets, and puts a live course board on every rider's phone — one QR code at the in-gate.

Open the designer See a live course board free · runs in your browser · installs like an app · works offline
01 — GENERATE

Pick the ring, the level, the type

RingCraft deals a stride-correct course from your actual jump inventory — hunter classics, jumper tracks with jump-offs, equitation tests — distances computed for the stride you school.

02 — REFINE

Drag until it rides right

Move and rotate fences, bend the track by hand, measure any line. Distances re-walk themselves live, and rulebook checks flag anything a steward would.

03 — PUBLISH

Print the pack, post the board

Rider, jump-crew and judge sheets on letter paper. One save puts the course on your live rider board — riders scan the QR and carry the plan in their pocket.

Built for show mornings

Design

Stride-correct by construction

Lines land on real distances — 12' stride or the pony stride you actually ride — with related distances labeled in strides and feet or meters.

Design

One layout, many courses

Set the jumps once, then route every division over them. Save layouts, re-route by clicking, and keep the crew from moving a single standard.

Print

The whole show pack

Rider plan with QR, crew sheet with heights, spreads and photos, judge sheet with time allowed — crisp on any letter printer, batch-printed per layout.

Share

Riders scan, nothing installs

A live board lists every published course. Edit the track and every phone sees it on refresh. Locked snapshot links freeze a course forever.

Anywhere

Installs like an app

Add it to your home screen for full-screen, offline course design — the board keeps working on barn Wi-Fi that isn't.
